# Provenance

`Suns/Basic.lean` and the three files in `FalseControls/` were written for this repository from
*Transient Structure at Solar Rigidity Interfaces and the Exceptional Point of the Dynamo Wave*
(v1.5, 16 August 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21895551). The paper grades its results as
computations; the statements here are the exact normal forms and numbers those computations
rest on.
