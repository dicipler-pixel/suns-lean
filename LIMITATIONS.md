# What is not proved here

Lean proves exactly the statements written, under exactly the hypotheses written.

* Every result the paper grades [V] (the shear-layer scans, the modal quench, the Kreiss curve,
  the Parker dynamo operator and its EP at `α₀* = 3.872`, the constant 1.853, the EP ladder
  census, the Parker–Yoshimura flip) is a computation, not a Lean proof.
* The square-root law is proved for the normal form `J(δ)`, whose constant is `1/2`. The
  paper's constant 1.853 belongs to its dynamo operator and is measured.
* The integer record is proved for the square-root branch along the loop. The winding number of
  the discriminant is not formalized.
* The hair-rate ladder is proved for the 2×2 Jordan-type normal form. The paper's operator and
  its numbers (−0.063, 3.5, +5.84) are computed.
* The predicted growth rates (0.0137–0.0612 s⁻¹) use measured apparent speeds and are not
  formalized.
